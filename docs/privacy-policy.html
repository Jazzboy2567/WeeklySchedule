Privacy Policy — Weekly Scheduler

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Weekly Scheduler ("the app") is a personal weekly planner for Android. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network connections.

Data you create

The schedule blocks you create (day, time, and title) and your settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. This data never leaves your phone and is removed if you uninstall the app.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications — to show what you should be doing at the current time.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) — to trigger those notifications at the right moment.
  • Run at startup — to restore your reminders after the phone reboots.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit data.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, trackers, or advertising, and does not send data to any third party.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

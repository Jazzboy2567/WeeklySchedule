## Full description (max 4000)
Weekly Schedule is a simple, private weekly planner. Lay out your whole week on a
calendar-style grid and let the app tell you what you should be doing right now — with a
notification that stays put until the task is done.

HOW IT WORKS
• Tap the grid to drop a time block, then drag its edges to fit the exact hours — and drag
  it sideways to repeat it across several days at once (for example, "Shower" 8–9 AM every
  day from Sunday to Saturday).
• Give each block a name and it repeats every week automatically.
• See your whole week at a glance, Sunday to Saturday, across a full 24-hour timeline.

STAY ON TRACK
• Locked mode: an ongoing notification that can't be swiped away, shown for the whole task.
• Reminder mode: a normal notification that alerts you when a task starts and repeats on an
  interval you choose.

MADE TO BE SIMPLE
• Light and dark themes.
• Clean, distraction-free design.
• Works completely offline.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking.
• Everything you create stays on your device. The app makes no internet connections.

Plan your week once, and let Weekly Schedule keep you on it.
